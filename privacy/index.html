Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DaMar Games Studios handles information in connection with our mobile games and this website.

Information processed by our games

Our games may store gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device. Some games may also use third-party advertising services. Those services may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising data, app interaction data and diagnostic information, depending on your device settings, consent choices and applicable law.

Advertising

Some of our games use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process data to provide, measure and personalize ads where permitted. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where required, advertising and data-processing settings should be configured appropriately for the intended audience and region.

Website data

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of operating the hosting service. We do not use an account system on this website.

Data retention

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted by the app, removed through app settings where available, or removed when you uninstall the app. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data, including access, deletion, correction or objection to certain processing. Device-level privacy and advertising controls may also be available in iOS or Android settings.

Third-party services

Our apps may link to or use services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our support page.
