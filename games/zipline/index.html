How to play

Start at the first numbered point and draw a continuous path through the remaining points in order. The route cannot pass through blocked cells, and directional cells can only be crossed in the direction they allow. Complete the level by covering every available cell with a valid path.

Progressive difficulty

Early levels teach the core logic, while later boards introduce larger grids, tighter routes and more demanding constraints.
